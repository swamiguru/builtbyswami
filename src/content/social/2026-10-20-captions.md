```
A five-second Instagram check before you trust any account selling you something.

Open the profile, tap the three dots at the top, then About this account.

It shows when the account joined and which country it's based in, and on many accounts, how often it changed its username. A 'Ministry scheme' page that joined in September from abroad with four old names isn't the Ministry.

Not every account shows every field, but most scam pages give themselves away.
```

```
The PM didn't post that video.

The MHA's cybercrime unit (I4C) flagged it on Monday: deepfake videos of the PM, the Finance Minister and state CMs are running as ads on Facebook and Instagram, selling welfare schemes that don't exist.

How it plays out:
You're "selected" for aid or cashback.
Someone calls or WhatsApps you.
First a registration or processing fee.
Then a scratch card that asks for your UPI PIN.

What's actually true:
No real scheme charges a fee to pay you.
A UPI PIN only ever sends money.
Calls from +92 numbers were specifically flagged.

Check the department's official website. If money's gone, call 1930 or file at cybercrime.gov.in straight away.

Save this + follow @longpressnews
```
