## POST 4 — The pre-mortem AI prompt (AI Workflow / Prompt of the Day · CAROUSEL)

### X (@builtbyswami)
💡 Stop asking AI "should I do this?" — it'll just agree.

Ask this instead 👇

"I'm about to [decision]. Argue the strongest case AGAINST it. List 3 ways it fails + the one thing I'm not seeing. Be blunt."

Turns any AI into a devil's advocate. 🧠

#AI #Productivity

[Link in a REPLY: https://longpress.news]

### Threads (@longpressnews)
