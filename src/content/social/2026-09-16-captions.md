## POST 5 — Myth: 200MP ≠ better photos (Myth-buster · SIMPLE · OPINION)

### X (@builtbyswami) — card_5
🔥 Myth: more megapixels = better photos.

Techtember phones flex 200MP, even 250MP. But those shots pixel-bin down to ~12-16MP anyway.

What decides your photo: sensor size, processing, lens & light — not the big number on the box. 📱

#Smartphones #Photography

### Threads (@longpressnews) — card_5
🔥 Killing a myth this Techtember: more megapixels ≠ better photos.

Phones now flex 200MP, even 250MP sensors. Sounds huge — but almost every shot gets pixel-binned down to ~12-16MP anyway. The 200MP mode is a headline, not your daily photo. 📸

What actually matters: sensor size, processing, the lens, and light.

Chasing the megapixel number is just how brands upsell you.

Ever shoot in full-res mode? 👇
