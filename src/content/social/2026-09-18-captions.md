### Threads (@longpressnews) — card_3

Built in, no app, works on most Androids.

Truth: it only stops YOUR device from saving history & cookies.

Not a disguise. Use it for what it's for. #PrivacyTips

(Reply with link: https://longpress.news)

### Threads (@longpressnews) — carousel post_4 (4 slides)
🕵️ Let's kill a myth: "Incognito = anonymous." ❌

Private/Incognito mode only stops YOUR device from saving history, cookies and form data.

Who still sees you: your ISP, your office/college Wi-Fi, every site you open, and anything you sign into.

What it's actually good for: not saving history on a shared laptop, or a second-account login. That's it.

Real privacy on public Wi-Fi? A trusted VPN — but even that's no invisibility cloak. 🧢

What did you think it did? 👇 #PrivacyTips
