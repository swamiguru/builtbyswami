## Post 3 — Report scam numbers on Chakshu (INDIA · SIMPLE · Tips & Tricks)

#ScamAlert #SancharSaathi
```

### Threads (@longpressnews) — image: card_3
```
Blocking a scam number protects you. Reporting it protects the next person.

Sanchar Saathi app (or sancharsaathi.gov.in) > Chakshu. Pick call, SMS or WhatsApp, add the number, time and a screenshot, verify with OTP.

DoT checks it and can disconnect the sender. It's for attempts only. If money's already gone, call 1930.

Ever reported one?
