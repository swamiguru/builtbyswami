Most of your UPI payments probably don't need a PIN.

UPI Lite is a small on-device wallet loaded from your bank: up to Rs 5,000 balance, Rs 1,000 per payment, no PIN. Your statement shows one top-up instead of forty Rs 20 entries.

The trade-off is that anyone holding your open phone can spend that balance. Keep it low, and use regular UPI for rent and big buys.
