## POST 4 — Prompt of the day: the ruthless editor (AI · SIMPLE)

### X (@builtbyswami)
💡 Prompt of the day — the ruthless editor:

"Act as a ruthless editor. Cut this by 40% without losing my meaning or voice. Give the tighter version first, then list what you cut and why."

Paste any email or caption. Watch the fluff vanish. ⚡️ #AI #Productivity

[Image: card_4 hook]

### Threads (@longpressnews)
💡 Steal this prompt — I use it on almost everything I write:

"Act as a ruthless editor. Cut this by 40% without losing my meaning or voice. Give me the tighter version first, then a short list of what you cut and why: [paste text]"

Works on emails, captions, messages, docs. The "what you cut and why" part is the trick — you learn your own filler. ⚡️

Try it on your next long email. What's your go-to prompt? #AI #Productivity
