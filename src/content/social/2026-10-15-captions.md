## Post 3 — WhatsApp: Block unknown account messages (GLOBAL, SIMPLE, Tips) — card_3

### X (@builtbyswami)
```
WhatsApp tip: Settings > Privacy > Advanced > Block unknown account messages. When a number you've never saved floods you with messages, WhatsApp blocks it automatically. Won't catch every scam, but it kills the spam bursts. #WhatsApp #Tips
```

### Threads (@longpressnews)
```
Ten-second WhatsApp fix most people haven't touched:

Settings > Privacy > Advanced > Block unknown account messages.

When an unknown account blasts you with messages, WhatsApp blocks it before your chat list fills up. It's a volume filter, so one careful scam message can still land.

Pair it with Settings > Privacy > Calls > Silence unknown callers. Then do the same on your parents' phones.

Switched it on yet?
#WhatsApp #Tips
```
