```
Small Mac trick I use almost every night.

Hold Option + Shift and press the volume or brightness keys. Each press moves a quarter of a normal step, so 16 levels turn into 64.

It solves the annoying thing where one notch is too loud for a 1 AM call and the notch below it is basically silent. Same deal for screen brightness in a dark room.
```

## Post 5 - Myth-buster: dark mode and battery (CAROUSEL, INSTAGRAM PICK) - opinion, most saveable

**X (@builtbyswami)** - image: card_5.png

```
Myth: dark mode saves a ton of battery. Purdue tested OLED phones. At 100% brightness it cut display power 39-47%. At the 30-50% most of us use, just 3-9%. On LCD, close to nothing. Use it because you like it. #DarkMode
```

**Threads (@longpressnews)** - image: card_5.png (or carousel post_5 slides 1-4)

```
Dark mode is not your battery fix.

Purdue researchers measured it on OLED phones. At full brightness, dark mode cut display power by 39-47%. At the 30-50% brightness most of us actually sit at, the saving fell to 3-9%.

On an LCD screen the backlight stays on regardless, so it does close to nothing.

Keep dark mode because it's easier on your eyes at night. If battery is the problem, look at brightness, screen-on time and weak signal.
#DarkMode #Battery
```
