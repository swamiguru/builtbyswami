🔌 Myth: "Wireless charging destroys your battery."

Truth: the villain isn't wireless charging — it's heat. 🔥

Wireless runs a little warmer than a cable, but modern phones + Qi2/MagSafe manage it just fine. Your battery won't notice in normal use.

The actual battery killers:
🔥 Cheap, misaligned charging pads
🧥 Thick cases trapping heat
🚗 Charging in a hot car
