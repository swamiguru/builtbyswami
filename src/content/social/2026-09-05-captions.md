## POST 3 — Tips & Tricks: Windows 11 Snipping Tool Text Actions (OCR) — SIMPLE
Card: card_3 (hook) — new tip, platform Windows 11, logged to used-tips-log.md

### X
💡 Windows 11 has a hidden text scanner — no app needed.

Hit Win + Shift + S, snip anything (image, PDF, video frame), then tap "Text Actions" → Copy all text. 📋

Runs locally, can even auto-redact emails & numbers. 🔒

Copy text from any screenshot in 2 seconds. ⚡️ #Windows11 #TechTips

### Threads
💡 Windows 11 has a built-in text scanner most people never touch.

Press Win + Shift + S → snip any image, PDF or paused video → click "Text Actions" → Copy all text. 📋

✅ Works fully offline (OCR runs on your PC)
✅ Can copy as a table
✅ "Quick Redact" auto-hides emails & phone numbers 🔒

No extension, no upload. Copy text off anything in seconds. ⚡️

Knew this one? 👇 #Windows11 #TechTips
