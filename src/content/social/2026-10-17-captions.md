## Post 3 — ChatGPT Ad Controls tip (GLOBAL app, India-relevant · SIMPLE · card_3)

### X (@builtbyswami)
```
On ChatGPT Free or Go? Settings > Ad Controls > turn off Personalize ads. Then turn off Past chats and memory, and tap Delete ads data.

Two minutes, and your old chats stop picking your ads.

#ChatGPT #TechTips
```

### Threads (@longpressnews)
```
ChatGPT ads are live in India on Free and Go. With personalization on, your past chats and memory can shape what you're shown.

Two-minute fix:
Settings > Ad Controls
Turn off Personalize ads
Turn off Past chats and memory
Tap Delete ads data

Ads stay, but they only use the chat you're in.

Did you know this setting existed?

#ChatGPT #TechTips
```
