Settings > Apple Pencil > Scribble. ⚡️

#iPad #TechTips

### Threads (@longpressnews) — card_3 (hook card)
💡 An iPad trick hiding in plain sight:

With Apple Pencil, you can handwrite directly into ANY text field — Safari's address bar, search boxes, forms — and iPadOS converts it to typed text on the fly. ✍️

Scratch out a word to delete it. Draw a vertical line to add a space.

Turn it on: Settings > Apple Pencil > Scribble.

Faster than reaching for the keyboard every time. Why isn't this front and centre? 👀
