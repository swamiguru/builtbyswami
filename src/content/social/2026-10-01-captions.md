## Post 4 — Check the SIMs in your name on Sanchar Saathi (CAROUSEL, INSTAGRAM PICK) — Security & Privacy quick-win, India, most saveable

**X (@builtbyswami)** — image: card_4_hook_2026-10-01.png (any link goes in a REPLY)

```
Someone could have a SIM on your ID and you'd never know. Check in two minutes: Sanchar Saathi app or sancharsaathi.gov.in > Know Mobile Connections in Your Name > OTP. Don't recognise one? 'Not my number'. Old SIM? 'Not required'. #CyberSafety #India
```

**Threads (@longpressnews)** — image: carousel post_4_2026-10-01_slide1-4.png in order

```
One for today. A lot of scam calls run on SIMs issued with someone else's KYC, and the person whose name is on them usually has no clue.

The DoT's Sanchar Saathi app and site list every mobile number tied to your ID. Know Mobile Connections in Your Name, enter your number, OTP, done.

Don't recognise one? Mark it 'Not my number'. An old SIM you stopped using gets 'Not required'.

Did anything surprise you?

#CyberSafety #SancharSaathi
```
