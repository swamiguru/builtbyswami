## Post 3: iPhone Stolen Device Protection on Always (Tips & Tricks, SIMPLE)

**X (@builtbyswami)** — image card_3_hook_2026-10-10.png; any link goes in a reply

```
Before the festive crowds: iPhone Settings > Face ID & Passcode > Stolen Device Protection, then set Require Security Delay to Always. Someone who shoulder-surfed your passcode still can't change your Apple Account password for an hour. #iPhone
```

**Threads (@longpressnews)** — card_3_hook_2026-10-10.png

```
If someone watches you type your iPhone passcode at a crowded mall, they can lock you out of your own Apple Account in minutes.

Fix: Settings > Face ID & Passcode > Stolen Device Protection. Turn it on, then set Require Security Delay to Always.

Now a password change needs Face ID, an hour's wait, then Face ID again. Default only kicks in away from home or work, which is why I'd pick Always.

Done it yet?
#iPhone #iOS
```
