Card: card_3 (hook) · Platform: Android · NEW tip (logged)

### X
💡 Android tip for "storage full" panic: turn on Auto-Archive.
Play Store → profile → Settings → General → Automatically archive apps. ⚡️
It removes apps you barely use but KEEPS your data + icon. One tap to restore — everything's where you left it. ✅
#AndroidTips #TechTips

### Threads
💡 Android hack for the "storage full" panic: Auto-Archive. ⚡️

Play Store → your profile → Settings → General → Automatically archive apps.

Instead of deleting, Android shrinks the apps you barely open — but keeps the icon AND your data. Tap once and it re-downloads exactly where you left off. 📲

Perfect if you're always at 0 MB before an update. No app, no root, free. ✅

Knew this one?
