## Post 5: Fake wallet extensions + browser clean-up (SIMPLE)
Why: Security & Privacy quick-win

```
16 fake Firefox extensions posing as Rabby and OKX wallets were grabbing recovery phrases, per Socket. All pulled by Oct 5.

Two-minute job tonight: open your extensions page and delete anything you don't recognise or haven't used in a month.

#CyberSecurity
```

### Threads (@longpressnews)
Image: card_5.png
```
Socket found 16 Firefox extensions dressed up as Rabby and OKX crypto wallets. When you imported a wallet, they copied your recovery phrase and sent it to the attackers. All were pulled by Oct 5.

No crypto? Same lesson. Type chrome://extensions or about:addons, look at the list, and remove anything you didn't knowingly install or haven't used in a month. Fewer extensions, fewer ways in.

How many are sitting in your browser right now?
```
