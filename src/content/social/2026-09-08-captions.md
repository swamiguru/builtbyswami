### Threads
💡 Stop shaking your iPhone to undo. 😂

Your screen has hidden 3-finger gestures for editing:

✌️ Swipe LEFT (3 fingers) = Undo
👉 Swipe RIGHT = Redo
🤏 Pinch IN (3 fingers) = Copy (pinch twice = Cut)
👐 Pinch OUT = Paste

Works in Notes, Mail, Messages — anywhere you type. Feels like magic once it's muscle memory. ⚡

Which one are you using first?
